Job Habitat

Find jobs where the usual work environment fits what you can handle, and that match what you like to do.

Before you start

  • This is not a test or a diagnosis. It is a starting point for exploring careers.
  • We do not ask for your name. Your answers are not saved.
  • There is no time limit. You can change any answer.
  • Preview build (4f5660d). Everything runs in your browser; your answers are never sent anywhere. The job numbers are made-up placeholders.
  • There are 14 short questions. Each one has a "Why we ask" note.

Part 1: Your work environment

Move each slider to show how much you can handle on a usual workday. Then say how much it matters to you.

Part 2: What you like to do

Rate how much you like each kind of work. If you have taken the free O*NET Interest Profiler on My Next Move (opens in a new tab), you can type your scores instead.

How do you want to give your interests?

Job ideas for you

Higher scores mean a closer fit with your answers. Open "Why this score?" to see what helped and what did not. Many workplaces can be changed with accommodations. The Job Accommodation Network (askjan.org) (opens in a new tab) has free, private advice.

    Job Habitat is a starting point for career exploration. It does not diagnose anything, it is not a personality test, and it cannot tell you what job you should do.
